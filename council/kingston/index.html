City of Kingston dog beaches

Victoria · 8 beaches in this tool.

Kingston’s foreshore dog maps are the summer PDF (1 November to 31 March) and the winter PDF (1 April to 31 October), both still linked from the dog ownership page. The maps were last marked updated June 2020. Dogs are banned all year on the signed no-dog zone from Mordialloc Creek to the end of Bay Street. The Beach Road shared path is still open to dogs on a leash.
