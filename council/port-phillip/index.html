City of Port Phillip dog beaches

Victoria · 6 beaches in this tool.

Port Phillip publishes dog beach rules for 1 November to 31 March and for 1 April to 31 October. Summer limits dogs to signed stretches of sand, and some of those stretches are only open from 7:30pm to 10am. The pin is the beach, not the boundary between groynes.
