Frankston City Council dog beaches

Victoria · 3 beaches in this tool.

Frankston’s summer beach ban starts on 1 December, not 1 November, and runs to 31 March. Outside 9:30am to 7:30pm in those months, and at all times from 1 April to 30 November, dogs are on a leash. Keast Park’s off-lead sand is the exception.
