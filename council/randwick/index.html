Randwick City Council dog beaches

New South Wales · 5 beaches in this tool.

Randwick’s off-leash areas are parks, not beaches. The off-leash page, updated 10 January 2025, says dogs are not permitted on Council’s beaches, and the lifeguards page says dogs are prohibited on all Council beaches. Coogee, Clovelly, Maroubra, Malabar and Little Bay are the beaches that page names.
