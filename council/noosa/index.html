Noosa Shire Council dog beaches

Queensland · 5 beaches in this tool.

Noosa’s beaches page names South Peregian as on leash, and North Sunshine Beach and Doggy Beach at Noosa Spit as off leash. Subordinate Local Law No. 2 also lists Marcus Beach from access 38 to 47 as off leash, and prohibits animals in bathing reserves and at the Noosa North Shore river-mouth habitat. Noosa Main Beach is not named in that schedule, so it stays on check with council.
