City of Port Adelaide Enfield dog beaches

South Australia · 5 beaches in this tool.

During daylight saving, the entire foreshore is a dog on-leash area from 10am to 8pm. The background page does not state what applies outside those hours, or during standard time, so those times stay on check with council. The hours follow daylight saving, not fixed October to March dates. A 2022 decision that would have split the beaches was rescinded on 30 August 2022 and is not encoded.
