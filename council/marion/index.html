City of Marion dog beaches

South Australia · 2 beaches in this tool.

Marion does not set beach hours. On its beaches a dog must be under effective control. The only beaches it names are Marino and Hallett Cove. Dogs are not allowed in Hallett Cove Conservation Park, which includes beach, so Hallett Cove stays on check with council.
