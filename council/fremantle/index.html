City of Fremantle dog beaches

Western Australia · 5 beaches in this tool.

Rules follow the adopted Council Policy on dog exercise and prohibited areas, amended 24 June 2026 (C2606-10). The April 2026 draft is not used. Leighton Beach north of the 209 metre line, South Beach from Douro Road to Scott Street, North Fremantle Foreshore and Rocky Bay Reserve are off lead. Port and Leighton south of that line, Harvest Road Beach, Rocky Bay opposite the Rule Street car park, and South Beach south to the groin are prohibited. Rule Street itself is subject to agreement with the state and is not encoded.
