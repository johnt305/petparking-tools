Town of Cottesloe dog beaches

Western Australia · 2 beaches in this tool.

The dog beaches page names South Cottesloe and Grant Street. Grant Street bans dogs from October to March, 10am to 4pm, and does not say what applies outside that window. South Cottesloe is called a dog beach and is not given a leash rule. Both stay on check with council.
