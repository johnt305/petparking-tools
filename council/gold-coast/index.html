City of Gold Coast dog beaches

Queensland · 12 beaches in this tool.

The Animal Control Area Register names The Spit, Marine Stadium, Palm Beach South, Tallebudgera Beach, Labrador foreshore and Paradise Point foreshore as off-leash areas, and Biggera Waters foreshore as off leash only from 5am to 8am and 4pm to 7pm. Outside a signed off-leash area a dog is on a leash. Tugun Beach is not in that register as an off-leash beach.
