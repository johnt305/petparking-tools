City of Stirling dog beaches

Western Australia · 3 beaches in this tool.

Stirling’s dog beaches are Peasholm Street at Scarborough, Trigg Beach between Kathleen and Bennion streets, and North Beach between Castle and Margaret streets. Dogs are prohibited at all other beach and coastal reserve areas.
