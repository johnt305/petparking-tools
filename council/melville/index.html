City of Melville dog beaches

Western Australia · 3 beaches in this tool.

Point Walter Reserve, Bicton Baths Reserve and Blackwall Reach Reserve are prohibited. Dogs are not permitted at foreshore reserves unless the reserve is designated as a restricted area, which on this page means on a leash. Melville does not name a dog exercise beach.
