Mornington Peninsula Shire dog beaches

Victoria · 11 beaches in this tool.

Bass Strait beaches between Flinders and Portsea are in Mornington Peninsula National Park and Point Nepean National Park and are dog prohibited all year. Bay beaches stay on check with council. The Dogs in Public Places Policy (adopted 25 June 2024) is about how areas are assessed, not a timetable. The leash-free PDF still says “Effective as at January 2013”, and Council changed controls on 12 December 2024, so that PDF is not used as the current rule. The interactive map did not publish a text schedule for Mornington, Mount Martha, Safety Beach, Dromana, Rosebud, Rye, Sorrento or Portsea front beach.
