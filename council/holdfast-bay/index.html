City of Holdfast Bay dog beaches

South Australia · 4 beaches in this tool.

During daylight saving, dogs on the beach must be on a lead between 10am and 8pm. The page does not say what applies outside those hours, or during standard time. Hooded plover breeding signs require a leash where they are displayed. Off-leash exercise on this page is described for reserves, not for the beach.
