Brisbane City Council dog beaches

Queensland · 4 beaches in this tool.

Brisbane City Council lists three designated foreshore off-leash areas: Manly, Sandgate at Shorncliffe, and part of Tuckeroo Park at Nudgee Beach. The former Nudgee Beach foreshore off-leash area is now on a leash. Outside the designated areas, dogs must be on a leash. The pin is a guide to the suburb, not the rock wall or the stair line.
