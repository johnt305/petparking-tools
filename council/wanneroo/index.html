City of Wanneroo dog beaches

Western Australia · 3 beaches in this tool.

Wanneroo names three dog exercise beaches: part of Quinns Rocks north from Tapping Way, part of Yanchep south from Nautical Court, and part of Two Rocks south of the southern groyne. Other beach reserve is prohibited. A dog may be walked on a lead on the constructed coastal pathways.
