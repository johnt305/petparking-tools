Closures and seasonal cautions

These notices come from council pages read separately from the by-laws. A closure turns the live status into “check with council”. A caution keeps the clock but replaces “all day” with the exception.

  • The Spit, Seaway rock groyne to Muriel Henchman Avenue: closed. The City of Gold Coast says The Spit North and The Spit South are closed until further notice because of erosion and sand scarps from Tropical Cyclone Alfred and seasonal high tides. Check with the council before you go. The Spit North, City of Gold Coast, checked 28 October 2026.
  • Aldinga Beach, Stairs north of Butterworth Road to Ocean Street: caution. Hooded Plover breeding season (August to March): dogs must be on a leash within 100 metres of any sign saying Hooded Plovers may be present, and are prohibited within 20 metres of a breeding site. Council lists Aldinga Beach and Snapper Point/Aldinga North among its regular breeding sites. This clock cannot see signs. Hooded Plovers, City of Onkaparinga, checked 28 October 2026.
  • Aldinga Beach, Aldinga foreshore outside the exercise area: caution. Hooded Plover breeding season (August to March): dogs must be on a leash within 100 metres of any sign saying Hooded Plovers may be present, and are prohibited within 20 metres of a breeding site. Council lists Aldinga Beach and Snapper Point/Aldinga North among its regular breeding sites. This clock cannot see signs. Hooded Plovers, City of Onkaparinga, checked 28 October 2026.

Still incomplete

Randwick’s named beaches and Bayside NSW’s Kyeemagh gates are in this version. These council pages were read on 27 October 2026 and still do not support a live status: Mornington Peninsula bay beaches (the interactive map has no text schedule, and the leash-free PDF says January 2013 while controls changed in December 2024), Surf Coast at Torquay, Jan Juc and Anglesea (the regulations page says timeshare applies on some popular beaches in summer and over Easter, and does not name the beach or the dates; the PDF returned HTTP 403), Wyndham’s Werribee South hours, Bayside Victoria winter times outside the Ricketts Point stretches named in the August 2025 statement, and Lady Robinsons Beach outside gates 60 and 61, where a 2021 PDF and the current page disagree.

Fremantle follows the adopted Council Policy on dog exercise and prohibited areas, amended 24 June 2026 (C2606-10). The April 2026 draft is not used. Holdfast Bay’s Animal Management Directions Plan 2022-2026 repeats the daylight-saving leash from 10am to 8pm and does not say what applies outside those hours, so those hours stay on check with council. Tugun Beach is not in the Gold Coast off-leash brochure. Palmerston lists dog parks and no ocean beach. Canberra is left out.

Councils with no beach encoded

These pages were read on 27 October 2026. They did not name a beach, stretch and rule that could be encoded, or the page could not be read.

  • Town of Cambridge. The Exercising Your Dog page says the Town has on-leash, off-leash, time-restricted and prohibited areas, and that the detail is in a Dog Exercise Areas Map PDF. The page text does not name City Beach or Floreat Beach, and the PDF was not a direct file link. No beach was encoded.
  • City of Charles Sturt. The dogs page and the dog-laws page returned HTTP 403. A council blog on retractable leads says certain beaches are on a lead between 10am and 8pm during daylight saving, and off lead outside those times under effective control, but it does not name Henley, Grange, Tennyson or West Beach. No beach was encoded.
  • City of Playford. The dogs page lists three fenced dog parks (Jo Gapper Park, Stebonheath Dog Park and Pooch Park) and does not name an ocean beach. No beach was encoded.
  • City of Salisbury. The on-and-off-leash table covers streets, wetlands, parks, fenced dog parks and St Kilda Playground. It does not state a beach rule. St Kilda Playground is not the beach. No beach was encoded.
  • Glenorchy City Council. The dog exercise page says dogs are off leash only in designated areas on the Discover Glenorchy map, and on a lead on foreshore outside those areas. It does not name a beach. A single foreshore pin would hide any mapped off-leash foreshore. No beach was encoded.
  • City of Perth (Mounts Bay). The rangers page names JH Abrahams Reserve at Crawley as a dog exercise area, and that reserve is encoded. Mounts Bay is not named as a dog beach or a dog exercise area. No Mounts Bay pin was added.
