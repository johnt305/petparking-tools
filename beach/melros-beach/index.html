Melros is split into dog exercise stretches, where a dog may be on or off leash, and stretches where dogs are prohibited.

This beach is published as more than one stretch. Read the one that matches the signs in front of you.

Florida Parade to Edwards Street

The status at the top of the page uses this stretch.

Live status loads in your browser.

Florida Parade to Edwards Street is a dog exercise area. The brochure legend says a dog exercise area is on or off leash, so off leash is allowed. Where there is no sign, dogs are on a lead.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.

Source: City of Mandurah page, checked 27 October 2026.

Melros Beach car park to 18 Oceanic Drive

Live status loads in your browser.

Melros Beach car park to 18 Oceanic Drive is a prohibited beach stretch. Dogs are not allowed.

All year
All day: no dogs.

Source: City of Mandurah page, checked 27 October 2026.

18 Oceanic Drive to the southern boundary

18 Oceanic Drive to the southern boundary is a dog exercise area. The brochure legend says a dog exercise area is on or off leash, so off leash is allowed. Where there is no sign, dogs are on a lead.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.

Source: City of Mandurah page, checked 27 October 2026.
