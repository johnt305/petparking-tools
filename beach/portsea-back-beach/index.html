Portsea Back Beach is on the Bass Strait coast between Flinders and Portsea. Parks Victoria national park. Dogs are prohibited all year.

Portsea Back Beach

The Shire says the Bass Strait coastline between Flinders and Portsea is managed by Parks Victoria as part of Mornington Peninsula National Park and Point Nepean National Park. These parks are dog prohibited at all times of the year. Council does not manage them.

All year
All day: no dogs.
