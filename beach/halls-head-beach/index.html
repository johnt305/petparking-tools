Halls Head is split into dog exercise stretches, where a dog may be on or off leash, and stretches where dogs are prohibited.

This beach is published as more than one stretch. Read the one that matches the signs in front of you.

Peel Inlet groyne to Janis Street

The status at the top of the page uses this stretch.

Live status loads in your browser.

Peel Inlet groyne to Janis Street is a dog exercise area. The brochure legend says a dog exercise area is on or off leash, so off leash is allowed. Where there is no sign, dogs are on a lead.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.

Source: City of Mandurah page, checked 27 October 2026.
