Collendina (access 7W to 8W): off leash all year, except on a leash within 75 metres of the 7W beach access.

Access 7W to 8W

The status at the top of the page uses this stretch.

Live status loads in your browser.

Dog control zone 7W to 8W: dogs must be on a leash within 75 metres either side of the Collendina 7W beach access. Dogs are permitted off leash under effective control on the rest of the zone, all year. The live status is the off-leash part of the zone. The 75-metre strip is on leash whenever you are in it.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.
