JH Abrahams Reserve at Crawley, also called Pelican Point, is a dog exercise area on the Swan River. Mounts Bay is not named.

JH Abrahams Reserve, Crawley

The status at the top of the page uses this stretch.

Live status loads in your browser.

The rangers page lists JH Abrahams Reserve, Crawley, as a dog exercise area, and says dogs must be on a lead except in a dog exercise area. The venue page calls it Pelican Point and a popular off-lead dog exercise area on the Swan River. Mounts Bay is not in that list.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.

Source: City of Perth page, checked 27 October 2026.

Also on the council page

  • The pin is the reserve at Pelican Point, not Mounts Bay.
  • A dog exercise area is not an exercise area during an organised function, sporting activity or event, or on a playground, thoroughfare or pathway. That exception is in Dog Local Law 2011 and is not a clock.

The map pin is a guide at -31.9820, 115.8240. It is not the boundary between groynes, access tracks or signed zones.
