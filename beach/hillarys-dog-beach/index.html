Hillarys Dog Beach is dedicated all year to exercising dogs. Dogs may be off lead inside the dog beach area and must remain under effective control. The page says it is the only beach where dogs can be exercised off lead. Dogs must be on a lead on the access path and in the car park. A temporary storm closure is not encoded as a standing ban.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.

Source: City of Joondalup page, checked 27 October 2026.

Also on the council page

  • Dogs must be on a lead on the dog beach access path, and in the car park. The horse beach immediately north is a separate entry.
