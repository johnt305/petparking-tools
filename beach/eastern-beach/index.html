Eastern Beach Reserve in Geelong is on leash unless a sign says otherwise. It is not part of the Barwon Coast dog orders.

Eastern Beach Reserve

The status at the top of the page uses this stretch.

Live status loads in your browser.

The Eastern Beach Reserve page says dogs are to be controlled on a leash in all public spaces unless otherwise signed, and lists the reserve as dogs on leash. The City’s dogs in public places page says beach foreshore and coastline areas are on leash except where designated otherwise.

All year
All day: on a leash. Outside those times: on a leash.
