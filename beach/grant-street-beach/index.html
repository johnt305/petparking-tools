Grant Street Beach is a dog beach at the end of North Cottesloe. Dogs are not allowed on the beach between October and March from 10am to 4pm. The page does not say whether dogs may be off leash or on a leash outside that window, or from April to September. Encoding only the ban would show “no dogs today” through the afternoon, which the page does not say. There is no live status.

Source: Town of Cottesloe page, checked 27 October 2026.
