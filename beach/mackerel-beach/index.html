Mackerel Beach, north of the public wharf: off leash sunrise to 9:30am and 5pm to sunset. South of the wharf is no dogs.

This beach is published as more than one stretch. Read the one that matches the signs in front of you.

North of the public wharf to Ku-ring-gai Chase National Park

The status at the top of the page uses this stretch.

Live status loads in your browser.

Dogs are permitted off leash from sunrise to 9:30am and from 5pm to sunset, from the public wharf north to Ku-ring-gai Chase National Park. At other times they are prohibited in that off-leash area, except on a leash using the shortest direct route between boats and the grassed strip. Sunrise and sunset are calculated for this pin.

All year
sunrise–9:30am: off leash. 5:00pm–sunset: off leash. Outside those times: no dogs.

Source: Northern Beaches Council page, checked 27 October 2026.

South of the public wharf

Dogs are prohibited at all times on Mackerel Beach south of the public wharf, and in the adjoining Ku-ring-gai Chase National Park.

All year
All day: no dogs.

Source: Northern Beaches Council page, checked 27 October 2026.
