Sandgate’s designated foreshore off-leash area is between the stairs to the foreshore near the corners of Flinders Parade and Second Avenue, and Park Parade and Zeehan Street at Shorncliffe. The page does not publish a clock. You must leash your dog outside the designated areas.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.
