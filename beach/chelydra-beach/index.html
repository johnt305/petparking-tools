Check with council

The marina FAQ lists Chelydra Beach, between the northern end of Port Coogee and the South Fremantle Power Station, as prohibited. The dog ownership page says dogs are prohibited at three locations only: Woodman Point, Coogee Beach and Ngarkal Beach. Chelydra is not in that list. The two current council pages do not agree, so there is no live status.
