This status is only for Wade Street to Priam Road. Each stretch below has its own status.

Show on the map

Silver Sands is split into dog exercise stretches, where a dog may be on or off leash, and stretches where dogs are prohibited.

This beach is published as more than one stretch. Read the one that matches the signs in front of you.

Wade Street to Priam Road

The status at the top of the page uses this stretch.

Live status loads in your browser.

Wade Street to Priam Road is a dog exercise area. The brochure legend says a dog exercise area is on or off leash, so off leash is allowed. Where there is no sign, dogs are on a lead.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.

Source: City of Mandurah page, checked 27 October 2026.

Henson Street car park to Stewart Street

Henson Street car park to Stewart Street is a dog exercise area. The brochure legend says a dog exercise area is on or off leash, so off leash is allowed. Where there is no sign, dogs are on a lead.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.

Source: City of Mandurah page, checked 27 October 2026.

Adonis Road to Henson Street

Live status loads in your browser.

Adonis Road to Henson Street is a prohibited beach stretch. Dogs are not allowed.

All year
All day: no dogs.

Source: City of Mandurah page, checked 27 October 2026.

Stewart Street west to the sea wall
