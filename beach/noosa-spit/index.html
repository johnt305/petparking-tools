Doggy Beach at Noosa Spit is off leash. The river-mouth sand known as the frying pan is a separate prohibited habitat.

This beach is published as more than one stretch. Read the one that matches the signs in front of you.

Noosa Spit, Woods Park to the river mouth

The status at the top of the page uses this stretch.

Live status loads in your browser.

The beaches page says dogs are permitted off leash at Doggy Beach at Noosa Spit. Schedule 7 describes Noosa Spit Reserve as access from Claude Batten Drive between Woods Park and the river mouth, and designates it as a dog off-leash area with no clock.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.

Source: Noosa Shire Council page, checked 27 October 2026.

Noosa North Shore river mouth, the frying pan

Schedule 6 prohibits all animals on the beach foreshore at the Noosa river mouth, adjacent to the area known as the frying pan, over the entire sand area identified by signs and barriers. That is a different place from Doggy Beach.

All year
All day: no dogs.

Source: Noosa Shire Council page, checked 27 October 2026.
