This status is only for Mona Vale Beach (south) trial area. Each stretch below has its own status.

Show on the map

Mona Vale Beach south has an off-leash trial until 13 October 2027. In daylight saving: 5:30am–10am and 5pm–9pm. In standard time: 6am–10am and 4pm–7pm. The rest of Mona Vale Beach is no dogs.

This beach is published as more than one stretch. Read the one that matches the signs in front of you.

Mona Vale Beach (south) trial area

The status at the top of the page uses this stretch.

Live status loads in your browser.

The off-leash trial runs from 14 October 2025 to 13 October 2027. In Australian Eastern Daylight Time, dogs are permitted off leash from 5:30am to 10am and from 5pm to 9pm. In Australian Eastern Standard Time, the hours are 6am to 10am and 4pm to 7pm, every day. At all other times in the trial period, dogs are prohibited in the trial area. After 13 October 2027, Council says dogs will be prohibited there 24 hours a day unless Council resolves otherwise. The two paths from South Mona Vale Headland are on leash.

Trial, daylight saving, until 13 October 2027
5:30am–10:00am: off leash. 5:00pm–9:00pm: off leash. Outside those times: no dogs.
Trial, standard time, until 13 October 2027
6:00am–10:00am: off leash. 4:00pm–7:00pm: off leash. Outside those times: no dogs.

All day: no dogs.
