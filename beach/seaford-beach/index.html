Seaford Beach: no dogs from 9:30am to 7:30pm between 1 December and 31 March. On a leash at every other time. November is still on leash all day.

From 1 December to 31 March, dogs are not allowed on the beach between 9:30am and 7:30pm. At all other times they are permitted on the beach on a leash. That includes all day from 1 April to 30 November. The Keast Park off-lead area is the exception. Frankston’s summer ban does not start on 1 November.

1 December to 31 March
9:30am–7:30pm: no dogs. Outside those times: on a leash.
1 April to 30 November
All day: on a leash.

Source: Frankston City Council page, checked 27 October 2026.
