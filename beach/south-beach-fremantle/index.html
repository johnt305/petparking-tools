This status is only for Douro Road to Scott Street. Each stretch below has its own status.

Show on the map

South Beach from Douro Road to Scott Street is off lead. From the southern boundary to Douro Road, including the playground, dogs are prohibited. The footpath beside the off-lead area is on a lead.

This beach is published as more than one stretch. Read the one that matches the signs in front of you.

Douro Road to Scott Street

The status at the top of the page uses this stretch.

South Beach Reserve (North), from the prolongation of the southern boundary of Douro Road to the prolongation of the southern boundary of Scott Street, is a dog exercise area, which means off lead. Playgrounds are excluded. It is on a lead during an authorised event.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.

An exercise area is on a lead while a Council authorised activity, function or sporting event, including team training, is being held. This clock cannot see events.

Source: City of Fremantle page, checked 27 October 2026.

South Beach Reserve (South), between its southern boundary and the prolongation of the southern boundary of Douro Road to the South Beach groin, including the playground, is prohibited. The footpath adjacent to the off-lead area is on a lead and is not part of this ban.

All year
All day: no dogs.

Source: City of Fremantle page, checked 27 October 2026.
