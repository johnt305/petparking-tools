Raby Bay Foreshore Park, between the first groynes east and west of Masthead Drive and Seacrest Court, is the off-leash exception. The rest of the Raby Bay foreshore is prohibited.

This beach is published as more than one stretch. Read the one that matches the signs in front of you.

Between the groynes at Masthead Drive and Seacrest Court

The status at the top of the page uses this stretch.

Raby Bay Foreshore Park on Masthead Drive is listed as an off-leash beach. The prohibited-places page says the allowed foreshore is in front of that park, between the first groyne east of Masthead Drive and Seacrest Court and the first groyne west of that intersection. No clock is published.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.

Source: Redland City Council page, checked 27 October 2026.
