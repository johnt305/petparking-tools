Howrah Beach is closed to dogs from 10am to 6pm from 1 December to 1 March, and off lead at other times.

Dogs are not permitted between 10:00am and 6:00pm from 1 December to 1 March each year. At all other times, dogs may be exercised off lead and must remain under effective control. On 1 March the daytime ban still applies. On 2 March it does not.

1 December to 1 March
10:00am–6:00pm: no dogs. Outside those times: off leash.
2 March to 30 November
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.

Source: Clarence City Council page, checked 27 October 2026.
