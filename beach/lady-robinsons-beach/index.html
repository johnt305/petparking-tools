Bayside Council NSW says the off-leash beach at Lady Robinsons Beach, Kyeemagh, between gates 60 and 61, is 24 hours. The pin is only a guide to Kyeemagh, not the gate line.

This beach is published as more than one stretch. Read the one that matches the signs in front of you.

Between gates 60 and 61, Kyeemagh

The status at the top of the page uses this stretch.

Live status loads in your browser.

The current off-leash page says a 24/7 off-leash beach area has been established at Lady Robinsons Beach, Kyeemagh, between gates 60 and 61. Dogs stay on a leash when entering or leaving that area. A council PDF from October 2021 is titled “Lady Robinsons Beach Off-leash Times 4pm – 10am” and says the area is open between 4pm and 10am. The May 2026 off-leash parks map names the gates and does not repeat a clock. This status follows the current page: off leash at all times between the gates. If the sign still shows 4:00pm to 10:00am, follow the sign and the current page together before you rely on the clock.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.

Lady Robinsons Beach outside gates 60 and 61

Check with council

The current off-leash page says dogs are prohibited, leashed or not, in bathing areas such as tidal pools, beaches and foreshores. The only beach it exempts is the area between gates 60 and 61. The October 2021 PDF for that off-leash area says dogs on a leash are permitted in all other areas. Those two council documents do not agree about the sand outside the gates, including the foreshore at Brighton-Le-Sands, Monterey and Ramsgate. There is no live status for that sand. Read the sign and the current page.

Source: Bayside Council (NSW) page, checked 27 October 2026.
