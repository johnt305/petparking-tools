This status is only for Nutgrove Beach, outside the Lipscombe Avenue stretch. Each stretch below has its own status.

Nutgrove Beach is off lead from 6pm to 10am in daylight saving and from 3pm to 10am otherwise. The Lipscombe Avenue stretch is on lead all day.

This beach is published as more than one stretch. Read the one that matches the signs in front of you.

Nutgrove Beach, outside the Lipscombe Avenue stretch

The status at the top of the page uses this stretch.

Live status loads in your browser.

Dogs are permitted off lead between 6pm and 10am during daylight saving, and between 3pm and 10am at all other times. Dogs are permitted on lead between 10am and 6pm during daylight saving, and between 10am and 3pm otherwise. The Lipscombe Avenue stretch is excluded from these hours.

Daylight saving: off lead 6:00pm to 10:00am
6:00pm–10:00am: off leash. Outside those times: on a leash.
Standard time: off lead 3:00pm to 10:00am
3:00pm–10:00am: off leash. Outside those times: on a leash.

Source: City of Hobart page, checked 27 October 2026.
