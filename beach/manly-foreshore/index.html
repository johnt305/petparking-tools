Manly foreshore, at the end of Davenport Drive between the southern and northern rock walls, is one of Brisbane’s three designated foreshore off-leash areas.

Between the southern and northern rock walls, Davenport Drive

The status at the top of the page uses this stretch.

Brisbane lists three designated foreshore dog off-leash areas. Manly is the beach area at the end of Davenport Drive, between the southern and northern rock walls. The page does not publish a clock for this area. You must leash your dog outside the designated areas.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.

Source: Brisbane City Council page, checked 27 October 2026.
