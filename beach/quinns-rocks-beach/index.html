Part of Quinns Rocks Beach, north from Tapping Way is a designated dog exercise beach. Off lead is allowed when the dog is under effective control. Other than Quinns Rocks, Yanchep and Two Rocks, dogs are prohibited on the beach reserve.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.
