The rules are written below.

This status is only for Access 47W to 50W. Each stretch below has its own status.

Show on the map

Breamlea access 47W to 50W is off leash all year. 44W to 47W and 50W to 52W follow wildlife signs, so those stretches say check with council.

This beach is published as more than one stretch. Read the one that matches the signs in front of you.

Access 47W to 50W

The status at the top of the page uses this stretch.

Live status loads in your browser.

Dogs are permitted off leash under effective control all year between 47W and 50W.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.

Source: City of Greater Geelong page, checked 27 October 2026.

Access 50W to 52W

Check with council

Between 50W and 52W, the zone map puts this stretch in the signage column with 44W to 47W: on a leash during periods set by signs, such as hooded plover nesting, and off leash under effective control at other times. Those dates are not a fixed calendar, so there is no live status.

Source: City of Greater Geelong page, checked 27 October 2026.

Access 44W to 47W

Check with council

Between 44W and 47W, on-leash periods are whatever the wildlife signs say at the time, including hooded plover nesting. Other times, dogs may be off leash under effective control. The dates are not a fixed calendar, so there is no live status.

Source: City of Greater Geelong page, checked 27 October 2026.
