Keast Park foreshore is Frankston’s year-round off-leash beach, about 200 metres of sand. It is excluded from the 9:30am to 7:30pm summer ban.

Keast Park off-lead sand

The status at the top of the page uses this stretch.

The summer beach ban excludes the Keast Park off-lead area. Council’s “A safer summer in Frankston” notice says the Keast Park free roam area is about 200 metres of sand off leash year-round. Greyhounds must stay on a leash.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.
