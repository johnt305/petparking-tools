Bondi Beach

Waverley Council says dogs must be on a leash at all times in Bondi Park, and dogs are prohibited on the beach, intertidal areas and ocean pools. Assistance animals are excepted. Off-leash areas in Waverley are parks, not Bondi Beach.

All year
All day: no dogs.
