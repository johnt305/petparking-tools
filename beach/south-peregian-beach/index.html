South Peregian Beach is on a leash. The local law describes Peregian Beach between access 59 at Grebe Street and access 67 at Pitta Street as a dog on-leash area.

Access 59, Grebe Street, to access 67, Pitta Street

The beaches page says dogs are permitted on a leash at South Peregian Beach. Schedule 7 Part 2 designates Peregian Beach between access 59 (Grebe Street) and access 67 (Pitta Street) as a dog on-leash area.

All year
All day: on a leash. Outside those times: on a leash.
