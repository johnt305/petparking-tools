This status is only for Bambra Street south to Mays Point. Each stretch below has its own status.

Show on the map

Roches Beach has three rules: south of Bambra Street is summer-restricted, Bambra Street north to the reef is on lead, and north of the reef towards Seven Mile Beach is off lead.

This beach is published as more than one stretch. Read the one that matches the signs in front of you.

Bambra Street south to Mays Point

The status at the top of the page uses this stretch.

Live status loads in your browser.

Roches Beach from Bambra Street south to Mays Point is restricted: no dogs from 10:00am to 6:00pm from 1 December to 1 March, and off lead at all other times.

1 December to 1 March
10:00am–6:00pm: no dogs. Outside those times: off leash.
2 March to 30 November
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.

Source: Clarence City Council page, checked 27 October 2026.

Bambra Street north to the reef opposite 11 Kirra Road

Live status loads in your browser.

Roches Beach from Bambra Street north to a point opposite 11 Kirra Road, from reef to reef, is an on-lead area.

All year
All day: on a leash. Outside those times: on a leash.

Source: Clarence City Council page, checked 27 October 2026.

Reef at 11 Kirra Road, north towards Seven Mile Beach

Live status loads in your browser.

The 1 December 2025 summer notice lists Roches Beach from the reef adjacent to 11 Kirra Road, north towards Seven Mile Beach, as an off-lead area. It is not in the restricted list.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.

Source: Clarence City Council page, checked 27 October 2026.
