The 4 December 2023 council decision formally declared Snug Beach south an off-lead dog exercise area. The consultation described it as sand only. No hours are published.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.

Source: Kingborough Council page, checked 27 October 2026.

Snug Beach outside the southern off-lead area

Check with council

The December 2023 decision adopts the southern off-lead area and does not restate a rule for the rest of Snug Beach. The 2018 notice of intention said Snug Beach would be restricted, and it was not a declaration. There is no live status for the rest of the beach.

Source: Kingborough Council page, checked 27 October 2026.
