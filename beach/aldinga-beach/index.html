The rules are written below.

This status is only for Stairs north of Butterworth Road to Ocean Street. Each stretch below has its own status.

Show on the map

From the stairs 100 metres north of Butterworth Road to the northern side of Ocean Street, dogs are off leash at any time. Outside that stretch, the daylight-saving 10am to 8pm leash applies. From August to March, Hooded Plover signs add a 100 metre leash zone and a 20 metre no-dog zone around breeding sites.

This beach is published as more than one stretch. Read the one that matches the signs in front of you.

Stairs north of Butterworth Road to Ocean Street

The status at the top of the page uses this stretch.

The Aldinga Beach dog exercise area, from an imaginary line west of the northern side of Ocean Street northerly to the stairs 100 metres north of Butterworth Road at Snapper Point, is off leash at any time under effective control.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.

Seasonal caution. Hooded Plover breeding season (August to March): dogs must be on a leash within 100 metres of any sign saying Hooded Plovers may be present, and are prohibited within 20 metres of a breeding site. Council lists Aldinga Beach and Snapper Point/Aldinga North among its regular breeding sites. This clock cannot see signs.

Council’s words: “Dogs are prohibited within 20 metres of a Hooded Plover breeding site. Dogs must be on a leash within 100m of any sign that indicates Hooded Plovers may be present in the area.” Source: Hooded Plovers, City of Onkaparinga, checked 28 October 2026.

Aldinga foreshore outside the exercise area

Foreshore that is not a named exercise area and not prohibited is on a leash from 10am to 8pm when daylight saving is in operation, and off leash under effective control outside those hours and during standard time. This stretch is Aldinga foreshore outside the Ocean Street to Snapper Point exercise area.

Daylight saving: on a leash 10:00am to 8:00pm, otherwise off leash
