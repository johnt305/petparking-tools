The Shire declares the northern foreshore of Peppermint Grove, Reserve 17113, the portion north of Leake Street to the shire boundary at Devil’s Elbow, as an exercise area at all times. An exercise area is supervised exercise without a leash. The reserves page places this dog exercise area in the natural bushland north of Leake Street, not on Freshwater Bay beach.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.

Source: Shire of Peppermint Grove page, checked 27 October 2026.
