Redland lists G.J. Walter Park, Middle Street, Cleveland, as an off-leash beach with beach access where dogs can go in the water. The seasonal shared-hours page names only Coochiemudlo Main Beach and Russell Island Lions Boulevarde Park, not this park. The page does not publish a clock.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.

Source: Redland City Council page, checked 27 October 2026.
