Check with council

Marion says the only beaches in the city are Marino and Hallett Cove, and that on beaches a dog must be under effective control, with no time limit. The same page says dogs are not allowed in Hallett Cove Conservation Park under state legislation. The park includes beach in its south-western corner. The page does not say where the council beach ends and the park beach begins. There is no live status.

Source: City of Marion page, checked 27 October 2026.
