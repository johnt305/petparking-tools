This status is only for Bayside Drive foreshore. Each stretch below has its own status.

Show on the map

Bayside Drive at Beachmere is off leash from 1 May to 31 July and on a leash from 1 August to 30 April. Other stretches at Beachmere have their own rules.

This beach is published as more than one stretch. Read the one that matches the signs in front of you.

Bayside Drive foreshore

The status at the top of the page uses this stretch.

Live status loads in your browser.

Bayside Drive is a seasonal dog off-leash and on-leash beach. Dogs can be exercised off leash on the foreshore of Bayside Drive between 1 May and 31 July, and on a leash between 1 August and 30 April. The on-leash season runs through the new year.

1 May to 31 July
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.
1 August to 30 April
All day: on a leash. Outside those times: on a leash.

Source: City of Moreton Bay page, checked 27 October 2026.

South of the second footbridge, Biggs Avenue

Live status loads in your browser.

Walk your dog off leash from the southern end of Biggs Avenue, across from the car park, south of the second footbridge. The page says this area is about 13 hectares at low tide, and dogs are allowed on the signed parkland and beach only. It does not give this stretch a season.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.

Source: City of Moreton Bay page, checked 27 October 2026.

Louise Drive to the northern end of Bayside Drive

Live status loads in your browser.

You can walk dogs on a leash from Louise Drive to the northern end of Bayside Drive. This is not the seasonal Bayside Drive foreshore and not the horses-only stretch.

All year
All day: on a leash. Outside those times: on a leash.

Source: City of Moreton Bay page, checked 27 October 2026.

Prince Street to the southern end of Biggs Avenue

Live status loads in your browser.

You can walk on a leash from Prince Street to the southern end of Biggs Avenue. The off-leash area starts south of the second footbridge, across from the car park.

All year
All day: on a leash. Outside those times: on a leash.

Source: City of Moreton Bay page, checked 27 October 2026.

Louise Drive beach entry to Brooks Road

Live status loads in your browser.

Horses can be exercised between the Louise Drive beach entry point and Brooks Road. No dogs are permitted in this area.

All year
All day: no dogs.

Source: City of Moreton Bay page, checked 27 October 2026.

Prohibited areas, including Beachmere Conservation Park

Live status loads in your browser.

No dogs or horses are allowed in the prohibited areas. These areas, including Beachmere Conservation Park, protect shorebird habitat. The page does not list every boundary in text, so this status is the signed prohibited areas, not the whole suburb.

All year
All day: no dogs.

Source: City of Moreton Bay page, checked 27 October 2026.
