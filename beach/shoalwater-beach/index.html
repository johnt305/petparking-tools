Shoalwater dog beach is one of nine off-leash dog beaches. Dogs are prohibited on every other Rockingham beach.

Arcadia Drive to the northern end of Mersey Point car park

The status at the top of the page uses this stretch.

Beach (accessed from Arcadia Drive) and ending at the northern end of the Mersey Point car park. The guide says these beaches are off-leash areas and the dog must be under effective control. Dogs are strictly prohibited on all other beaches. Wheelchair access from October to May, where the guide mentions it, is access, not a dog clock.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.

Source: City of Rockingham page, checked 27 October 2026.
