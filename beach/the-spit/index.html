The City of Gold Coast says The Spit is closed until further notice after erosion from Tropical Cyclone Alfred, so this page says check with council. When it reopens, the foreshore between the southern Seaway rock groyne and the Muriel Henchman Avenue beach entrance is an off-leash area, and the flagged bathing area at lifeguard tower 45 is excluded from the usual 200 metre flag ban.

The Animal Control Area Register designates The Spit, Sea World Drive, Main Beach, as a dog off-leash area: the foreshore between the southern Seaway rock groyne and the Muriel Henchman Avenue beach entrance, map 120, resolved 5 April 2013. Dogs are prohibited within 200 metres of any flagged bathing area, excluding the flagged bathing area inside this off-leash area at the beach known as The Southport Spit, adjacent to lifeguard tower 45.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.

The 200 metre flag ban does not apply to the flagged bathing area at lifeguard tower 45. It still applies to other flagged bathing areas. This clock cannot see flags.

Closed by council. The City of Gold Coast says The Spit North and The Spit South are closed until further notice because of erosion and sand scarps from Tropical Cyclone Alfred and seasonal high tides. Check with the council before you go.

Council’s words: “Beach closed: Due to severe erosion and sand scarps caused by Tropical Cyclone Alfred, along with seasonal high tides, access to the beach has been impacted. This beach will be closed until further notice.” Source: The Spit North, City of Gold Coast, checked 28 October 2026.
