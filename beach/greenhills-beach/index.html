Greenhills Beach, Cronulla: off leash 4pm to 10am in daylight saving, and 3pm to 10am in standard time. Prohibited outside those hours. Gate 5. Dogs stay on a leash while entering and leaving.

Greenhills Beach, north of Wanda, is the timed off-leash beach. Dogs are allowed off leash during daylight saving from 4pm to 10am, and outside daylight saving from 3pm to 10am. They are prohibited at all other times. Enter and leave on a leash. The entrance is Gate 5. Daylight saving here follows the Australia/Sydney clock.

Daylight saving time: 4pm to 10am
4:00pm–10:00am: off leash. Outside those times: no dogs.
Australian Eastern Standard Time: 3pm to 10am
3:00pm–10:00am: off leash. Outside those times: no dogs.

Source: Sutherland Shire Council page, checked 27 October 2026.
