Clontarf dog beach is on a leash only, on the beach stretch published by the City of Moreton Bay.

Woody Point Jetty to the Houghton Highway bridge

The status at the top of the page uses this stretch.

Dogs may be exercised on-leash only, on the beach area between the Woody Point Jetty and the Houghton Highway bridge.

All year
All day: on a leash. Outside those times: on a leash.
