Woorim Beach south is off leash on sand and water from beach access 19 at Lowry Street, south for about 4.2 kilometres. Signs then start an on-leash area.

This beach is published as more than one stretch. Read the one that matches the signs in front of you.

Dogs may be exercised off leash on sand and water only, from beach access 19 at Lowry Street, Woorim, heading south for about 4.2 kilometres. Dogs are not permitted on the dunes or in vegetated areas.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.

Source: City of Moreton Bay page, checked 27 October 2026.
