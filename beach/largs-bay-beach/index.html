During daylight saving, dogs on Largs Bay Beach are on a leash from 10am to 8pm. The council page does not state a clock outside those hours, or during standard time.

The project background page records the remaining council resolution: the entire foreshore is a dog on-leash area between 10.00am and 8.00pm on any day during daylight saving. It does not state an off-leash rule outside those hours, or a clock for standard time, so those times stay on check with council. The August 2022 spatial split of the beaches was rescinded on 30 August 2022.

Daylight saving: on a leash 10:00am to 8:00pm
10:00am–8:00pm: on a leash. Outside those times: not stated — check with council.
Standard time: the page does not state a beach clock
All day: not stated — check with council.

Source: City of Port Adelaide Enfield page, checked 27 October 2026.

Also on the council page

  • The on-leash hours follow South Australian daylight saving, not fixed October to March or April to September dates.
  • The background page does not say what applies before 10am, after 8pm, or while standard time is in force.
  • The 9 August 2022 decision that would have split these beaches was rescinded on 30 August 2022. It is not encoded.
