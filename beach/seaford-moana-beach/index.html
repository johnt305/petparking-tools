Seaford to Moana is a dog exercise area at any time, off leash under effective control.

Tiller Drive, Seaford, to Robertson Road, Moana

The status at the top of the page uses this stretch.

Tiller Drive, Seaford, to Robertson Road, Moana is excluded from the daylight-saving leash and is a dog exercise area at any time. Off leash is allowed under effective control. The 2022 by-law note says that outside a prohibited area, and outside the 10am to 8pm daylight-saving leash on foreshore the council has resolved, a dog may be exercised off leash under effective control.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.

Source: City of Onkaparinga page, checked 27 October 2026.
