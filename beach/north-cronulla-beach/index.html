Dogs are prohibited on North Cronulla Beach.

North Cronulla Beach

The status at the top of the page uses this stretch.

Live status loads in your browser.

Sutherland Shire says dogs are prohibited on beaches except designated off-leash areas. The listed beach off-leash areas are Greenhills, Horderns Beach at Bundeena, and Silver Beach at Kurnell. North Cronulla is not one of them.

All year
All day: no dogs.

Source: Sutherland Shire Council page, checked 27 October 2026.
