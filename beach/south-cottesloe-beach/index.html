The dog beaches page calls South Cottesloe a dog beach, from the small groyne at Beach Street to Mosman Beach. It does not say whether the dog is off leash or on a leash, and it does not publish hours. A 2020 designated-areas document uses a different boundary and was not used to fill that gap. There is no live status.
