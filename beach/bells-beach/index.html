Dogs can be walked at Bells Beach only on a leash, at all times. The adjoining Point Addis Marine National Park has its own rules.

Bells Beach Surfing Recreation Reserve

The status at the top of the page uses this stretch.

Surf Coast Shire says dogs can be walked on Bells Beach but must be on a leash at all times. The reserve page says dogs are only allowed in the Bells Beach Surfing Recreation Reserve on a leash, because of wildlife including the rufous bristlebird, and that the adjoining sandy beach is part of Point Addis Marine National Park.

All year
All day: on a leash. Outside those times: on a leash.

Source: Surf Coast Shire page, checked 27 October 2026.
