Marcus Beach from access 38 to access 47 is a dog off-leash area in Noosa’s animal management local law. No clock is published.

Access 38 to access 47

The status at the top of the page uses this stretch.

Live status loads in your browser.

Schedule 7 Part 1 designates dog off-leash areas. The Marcus Beach row is access 38 to access 47, with no hours. The dogs page links a Marcus Beach map under dog exercise areas.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.

Source: Noosa Shire Council page, checked 27 October 2026.
