This status is only for Westview Parade to Dawesville Cut. Each stretch below has its own status.

Show on the map

Wannanup is split into dog exercise stretches, where a dog may be on or off leash, and stretches where dogs are prohibited.

This beach is published as more than one stretch. Read the one that matches the signs in front of you.

Westview Parade to Dawesville Cut

The status at the top of the page uses this stretch.

Live status loads in your browser.

Westview Parade to Dawesville Cut is a dog exercise area. The brochure legend says a dog exercise area is on or off leash, so off leash is allowed. Where there is no sign, dogs are on a lead.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.

Source: City of Mandurah page, checked 27 October 2026.

Westview Parade north-east to Avalon Parade

Live status loads in your browser.

Westview Parade north-east to Avalon Parade is a prohibited beach stretch. Dogs are not allowed.

All year
All day: no dogs.

Source: City of Mandurah page, checked 27 October 2026.

Also on the council page

  • The pin is a guide to the suburb, not the signed boundary.
  • The brochure says that where there is no sign stating whether the place is an exercise area or a prohibited area, dogs are on a lead.

The map pin is a guide at -32.6120, 115.6420. It is not the boundary between groynes, access tracks or signed zones.
