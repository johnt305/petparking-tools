West Torrens says that, provided the dog is under effective control, you can exercise it off leash on the section of beach within West Torrens at any time of the day. Effective control means the dog is close, responds to verbal commands, and can be seen. Roads, shared paths and public places other than parks are on a leash.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.

Source: City of West Torrens page, checked 27 October 2026.
