This status is only for Northern 209 metres, to the district boundary. Each stretch below has its own status.

The northern end of Leighton Beach, from 209 metres south of the district boundary up to that boundary, is an off-lead exercise area. South of that line, Port and Leighton Beaches are prohibited.

This beach is published as more than one stretch. Read the one that matches the signs in front of you.

Northern 209 metres, to the district boundary

The status at the top of the page uses this stretch.

Live status loads in your browser.

Leighton Beach bounded on the south by a line 209 metres south of the northern district boundary, and on the north by that boundary, is a dog exercise area. A dog exercise area is off lead. It is on a lead during a Council authorised activity, function or sporting event, including team training.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.

An exercise area is on a lead while a Council authorised activity, function or sporting event, including team training, is being held. This clock cannot see events.

Source: City of Fremantle page, checked 27 October 2026.

Port and Leighton Beaches south of the 209 metre line

Live status loads in your browser.

Port and Leighton Beaches, from the prolongation of the southern end of Port Beach Road north to a line 209 metres south of the northern district boundary, are dog prohibited areas. Dogs are strictly prohibited, on or off lead. The circular grass reserve west of Freeman Loop is not included.

All year
All day: no dogs.

Source: City of Fremantle page, checked 27 October 2026.
