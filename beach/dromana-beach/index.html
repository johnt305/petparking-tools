Port Phillip Bay beaches on the Peninsula are managed by a mix of the Shire and the state Department of Energy, Environment and Climate Action. The Shire’s interactive map is the place it tells visitors to look. On 27 October 2026 that map did not yield a text rule for Dromana Beach. The leash-free PDF linked from the Shire still says “Effective as at January 2013”, and dog controls changed on 12 December 2024, so that PDF is not used. The Dogs in Public Places Policy adopted on 25 June 2024 does not list clock times. There is no live status.

Source: Mornington Peninsula Shire page, checked 27 October 2026.

The map pin is a guide at -38.3336, 144.9624. It is not the boundary between groynes, access tracks or signed zones.
