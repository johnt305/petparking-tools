Woorim Beach from the northern end of North Street to the 4WD access is on a leash only. Dogs are not permitted on the dunes.

North Street to the 4WD beach access

Dogs may be exercised on a leash only on the beach between the pedestrian entrance at the northern end of North Street and the 4WD beach access point. Dogs are not permitted on the dunes or in vegetated areas.

All year
All day: on a leash. Outside those times: on a leash.
