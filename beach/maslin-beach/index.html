This status is only for Broadbeach Drive north to Ochre Point. Each stretch below has its own status.

Show on the map

North from Broadbeach Drive to Ochre Point is off leash at any time. Between the lower car park at Maslin Creek and Blanche Point, dogs are on a leash from 10am to 8pm while daylight saving operates.

This beach is published as more than one stretch. Read the one that matches the signs in front of you.

The Maslin Beach dog exercise area, from the northern side of Broadbeach Drive northerly to Ochre Point, is off leash at any time under effective control.

Lower car park at Maslin Creek to Blanche Point

Live status loads in your browser.

The foreshore between the southern boundary of the lower car park adjacent to Maslin Creek and Blanche Point is on a leash from 10am to 8pm on any day when daylight saving is in operation. Outside those hours, and during standard time, the leash resolution does not apply, so off leash under effective control follows the by-law note.

Daylight saving: on a leash 10:00am to 8:00pm, otherwise off leash
10:00am–8:00pm: on a leash. Outside those times: off leash.
Standard time: off leash all day
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.

Source: City of Onkaparinga page, checked 27 October 2026.
