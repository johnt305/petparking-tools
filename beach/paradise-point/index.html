The foreshore beside Paradise Point Parklands, between the northern platform and The Sovereign Mile bridge, is off leash. The swimming area east of the footpath is prohibited.

This beach is published as more than one stretch. Read the one that matches the signs in front of you.

The register designates Paradise Point Foreshore, The Esplanade, the foreshore adjacent to Paradise Point Parklands between the northern platform and The Sovereign Mile bridge, as a dog off-leash area, map 129, resolved 30 July 2019.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.
