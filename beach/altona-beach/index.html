Altona Beach between Romawi and Mount Streets: no dogs 10am to 9:30pm from 1 November to 30 April. On a leash at all other times. Altona Dog Beach is a different stretch.

Council says dogs are restricted from Altona Beach between Romawi and Mount Streets from 10am to 9:30pm from 1 November to 30 April, seven days a week. At all other times, dogs must be on a leash. This tool treats the restricted hours as no dogs on that sand, because the page contrasts them with “at all other times, dogs must be on a leash.”

1 November to 30 April
10:00am–9:30pm: no dogs. Outside those times: on a leash.
1 May to 31 October
All day: on a leash.

Source: Hobsons Bay City Council page, checked 27 October 2026.
