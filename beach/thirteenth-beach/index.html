13th Beach dog rules depend on the access number. 40W to 42W is prohibited all year. 36W to 40W is on leash when horses are present.

This beach is published as more than one stretch. Read the one that matches the signs in front of you.

Access 36W to 40W, horse zone

The status at the top of the page uses this stretch.

Check with council

Between 36W and 40W, dogs must be on a leash when horses are present and may be off leash under effective control at other times. This tool cannot know whether horses are on the beach, so the status is check with council.

Source: City of Greater Geelong page, checked 27 October 2026.
