Rocky Bay Reserve, Harvest Road Beach to the district boundary

The status at the top of the page uses this stretch.

Live status loads in your browser.

Rocky Bay Reserve, bounded on the south by Harvest Road Beach, on the north by the northern district boundary, on the east by the Swan River and on the west by the reserve boundaries, is a dog exercise area, which means off lead, except during an authorised event. The beach opposite the Rule Street car park is a separate prohibited area.

All year
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.

An exercise area is on a lead while a Council authorised activity, function or sporting event, including team training, is being held. This clock cannot see events.

Source: City of Fremantle page, checked 27 October 2026.

Beach opposite the Rule Street car park

Live status loads in your browser.

Rocky Bay Beach, the beach bordering the Swan River opposite the Rule Street car park, North Fremantle, is a dog prohibited area. Rule Street at 36 Rule Street is listed separately and is subject to agreement with the Department of Planning, Lands and Heritage, so it is not given a status.

All year
All day: no dogs.

Source: City of Fremantle page, checked 27 October 2026.
