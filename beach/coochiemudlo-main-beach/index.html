Coochiemudlo Main Beach, Victoria Parade west of the boat ramp, is off leash 5pm to 9am in January, February, November and December, on a leash in March, April, September and October, and off leash at any time from May to August.

Victoria Parade, west of the boat ramp

Shared hours, timed to migratory birds. January and February, and November and December: off leash from 5pm until 9am the next day, and on a leash at other times, between the boundary signs. March and April, and September and October: on a leash at all times. May to August: off leash at any time between the boundary signs.

January and February
5:00pm–9:00am: off leash. Outside those times: on a leash.
March and April
All day: on a leash. Outside those times: on a leash.
May to August
All day: off leash. Outside those times: off leash.
September and October
All day: on a leash. Outside those times: on a leash.
November and December
5:00pm–9:00am: off leash. Outside those times: on a leash.
